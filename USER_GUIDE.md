# BUSE Teaching Practice Platform User Guide

This guide explains the main tasks available to students, mentors, supervisors, coordinators, and administrators. The options shown after sign-in depend on your assigned role.

## Sign In and Navigate

1. Open the portal address provided by your institution.
2. Enter your registered email and password, then select **Sign In**.
3. Use the left navigation to open your dashboard, messages, forms, or other role-specific pages.
4. Open the profile menu to edit your profile or log out.

If you do not have an account or cannot sign in, contact your coordinator or administrator. Do not share your password or registration token with other users.

## Everyone

### Update your profile

Open the profile page from the profile menu. You can update your name, title, phone number, programme, level, school, and other fields available for your role. Students can also maintain their class, teaching subject, TP topic, and TP dates. Select **Save Changes** to store updates.

To change your password, enter your current password and the new password twice in the **Change Password** section. The new password must be at least six characters.

### Send a message

Open **Messages**, select a contact, and send your message. Available contacts and conversations depend on your account and system permissions.

## Students

### Submit teaching practice documents

1. Open **Upload Documents** or select **Upload Document** on your dashboard.
2. Enter a clear title, choose the submission category, and add a description or target date if needed.
3. Select or drag files into the upload area. Supported types include Word documents, PDF, Excel, MP4, and JPG/PNG images.
4. Select **Upload submission** and wait for the success message.

Use the appropriate category for return slips, schemes of work, daily lesson plans, evidence, or reflections. Your registration number and profile information are attached automatically when available.

### Track submissions and feedback

The dashboard lists your submissions and their status. Use the status filter to narrow the list. Select the feedback icon on a submission to view reviewer comments and ratings when available. Pending submissions can be removed from your record.

### View progress and assessment results

Open **Progress** for your progress view. Open **Final Mark** to view the final assessment recorded by your supervisor; students cannot edit it. Open **Assessment Forms** to view forms that reviewers have submitted for you.

Announcements appear on the dashboard. Select an announcement to mark it as read.

## Mentors

### Review student work

Open **Dashboard** to view student submissions available to your account. Select a submission to open the feedback panel, choose a rating, enter constructive comments, and select **Submit Feedback**.

### Complete an assessment form

Open **Assessment Forms**, select the assigned student, and choose **Lesson Observation Instrument**. Complete the form and save or submit it using the actions provided on the form.

## Supervisors

### Review submissions

1. Open **Submissions** and select an assigned student.
2. Review the student name, registration number, programme, submission category, status, and submission date.
3. Use **Download** for an individual file, or **Download all submissions** for a ZIP archive.
4. Select **Approve** or **Reject** to update a submission's status.

### Enter a final mark

Open **Final Mark Entry**, choose an assigned student, complete the teaching practice details and rubric scores, then save the final mark. Check the student and scores before saving; the student view is read-only.

### Complete assessment forms

Open **Assessment Forms**, select an assigned student, and choose **Test Supervision and Assessment** or **Assessment of Documents**. Complete and save or submit the form using the actions provided.

## Coordinators

### Manage accounts

Open the dashboard's **Users** tab to search and filter accounts, activate or deactivate users, reset passwords, and delete eligible accounts. Use **Register User** to create an account directly. Enter the student's registration number when registering a student.

### Assign students

Use the **Assignments** tab to assign a student to a mentor and/or supervisor and specify the school. To assign several students, open **Bulk Assign** and provide CSV text with a header row such as:

```csv
studentId,mentorId,supervisorId,school
123,45,67,Example School
```

Use the user IDs shown by the system for `studentId`, `mentorId`, and `supervisorId`. Confirm that the processed row count is as expected.

### Announcements, reports, and gradebook

Use **Announcements** to create a message for all users or a selected role. Use **Reports** for system summaries and **Gradebook** to review marks. Coordinators can view, but not edit, final marks.

## Administrators

Administrators can perform coordinator tasks and have additional **Settings** access. Use Settings to manage system options and registration tokens. Share tokens only with their intended recipients and use an expiry appropriate to the registration period.

## Getting Help

If an expected page or action is missing, first confirm that you are signed in with the correct account. Contact your coordinator or administrator if your role, student assignment, or access needs to be corrected. Include the page name and the time of the problem, but do not send passwords or tokens.
